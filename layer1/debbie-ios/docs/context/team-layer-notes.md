Layer 1: Device Setup and Pairing
Turning an old laptop into a connected care hub with one command.
Install: User pastes one command; the installer downloads the hub and required runtimes.
Check hardware: Detect CPU, available RAM, storage, and supported acceleration.
Prepare models: Download and verify compatible models, with visible progress and automatic retries.
Show QR: Automatically open a setup page displaying an expiring, single-use pairing QR code.
Connect phone: Caregiver scans the QR and approves pairing; both devices establish an authenticated, encrypted connection.
Check sensors: Guide camera/microphone permissions and confirm sensor availability.
Start hub: Launch background services and show separate connection, model, and monitoring statuses.
Hand off to Layer 2: Register the device’s identity, capabilities, available resources, and connection details for ongoing device management.
Output: A paired, authenticated laptop ready to receive tasks—no manual dependency installation or configuration.
