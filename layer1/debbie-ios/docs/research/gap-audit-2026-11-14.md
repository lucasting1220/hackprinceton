## Delivery review: first-run failures and rule risks

- **blocker** `fastlane/Fastfile:124`: Capabilities are enabled with BundleId#update_capability, which is the developer-portal (web-session) form.   - Fix proposed: Add INFOPLIST_KEY_NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription, e.g. 'Debbie only reads your heart rate and never writes to Health.', and add the key to the loop at validate-ipa.sh:16.
